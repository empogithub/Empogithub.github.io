Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Welcome to Empo.cc. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and shared when you visit this website. It is intended to describe our approach to the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the ePrivacy Directive.

This policy applies to Empo.cc and the website features described below. Other websites linked from Empo.cc may have their own privacy policies and practices.

The website operator is responsible for deciding how and why personal data is processed on Empo.cc, where applicable under data protection law.

Website: https://empo.cc/
Privacy contact: Empo@empo.cc

Contact us if you have questions about this policy or wish to exercise your data protection rights.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the website and your privacy choices, the following information may be processed:

  • Technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages and server logs.
  • Analytics information, such as page visits and interactions, where analytics is enabled.
  • Cookie identifiers and advertising identifiers, where used by analytics or advertising providers.
  • Information you choose to include in an email sent to us.
  • Consent preferences relating to cookies and similar technologies.

The exact information collected depends on the services enabled, your browser settings and your consent choices. We do not claim to collect every category listed above on every visit.

3. Why We Process Information

  • To operate, secure and maintain the website.
  • To understand website usage and improve its content.
  • To display advertisements and support the operation of the website, subject to applicable consent requirements.
  • To respond to enquiries sent by email.
  • To remember and respect privacy and consent preferences.
  • To detect abuse, fraud, technical problems and security incidents.

4. Legal Bases Under GDPR

Where GDPR applies, personal data is processed only where a valid legal basis exists. Depending on the activity, this may include:

  • Consent: for non-essential cookies, tracking technologies and advertising or analytics activities where consent is required.
  • Legitimate interests: for proportionate website security, abuse prevention and essential operational activities, where permitted and after balancing relevant rights.
  • Pre-contractual steps: where necessary to respond to a request about website hosting or another proposed service.
  • Legal obligations: where processing is required by applicable law.

The legal basis must be determined for each actual processing activity. Legitimate interests do not replace consent where the ePrivacy rules require prior consent to store or access information on a user's device.

8. Cookies and the ePrivacy Directive

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for several purposes:

  • Strictly necessary: technologies required for a service or feature explicitly requested by the visitor, or necessary for permitted technical operation.
  • Analytics: technologies used to measure website traffic and understand visitor behaviour.
  • Advertising: technologies used to deliver, measure or personalise advertisements.

Under the ePrivacy rules as implemented in applicable national law, prior consent is generally required before storing or accessing information on a visitor's device, unless a relevant exemption applies. GDPR requirements also apply when personal data is processed.

Visitors must be able to accept or refuse non-essential technologies and change their preferences later. Refusing optional tracking should not be made unnecessarily difficult.

A browser's cookie controls can also be used to remove or block cookies. Blocking cookies may affect some website functionality.

9. Consent and Withdrawal

Where consent is required, it must be freely given, specific, informed and expressed through a clear affirmative action. Optional analytics and advertising technologies must not be activated before the necessary consent.

You can withdraw your consent at any time through the website's consent settings, where implemented. Withdrawal does not make processing that occurred before withdrawal unlawful.

The website should provide a functioning consent interface with separate choices where appropriate, including an option to reject non-essential technologies and a way to reopen the settings.

Some service providers may process information outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where personal data is transferred to a country without an applicable adequacy decision, the transfer must rely on an appropriate GDPR transfer mechanism and applicable safeguards, where required.

Contact us for further information about relevant safeguards, subject to applicable law and the information available to us.

12. Data Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

  • Emails: retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to and manage the enquiry, subject to applicable legal obligations.
  • Server logs: retained according to the hosting provider's configuration and operational or security requirements.
  • Analytics and advertising: retention depends on the relevant provider's settings and policies.
  • Consent records: retained as necessary to demonstrate and manage consent choices.

Exact retention periods should be specified once the settings and practices of each provider have been verified.

13. Your GDPR Rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request erasure of personal data.
  • Request restriction of processing.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Receive certain data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing relies on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with your competent data protection authority.

These rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions. To make a request, email Empo@empo.cc. We may need to verify your identity before responding.
